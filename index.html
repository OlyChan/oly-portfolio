About Me

I like things to make sense and to be in the right place.
I'm Chinenye, a Virtual and Executive Assistant with a background in Computer Science and a soft spot for organization, research, and figuring things out. My experience has taught me that good support goes beyond checking things off a list. It's about keeping track of the little details, following through on pending tasks, finding useful information, and making sure nothing important gets lost in the shuffle. I'm naturally detail-oriented, curious, and always looking for a more practical way to get things done. Give me a messy inbox, a list of moving parts, or a research task that needs narrowing down, and I'm happy to bring some order to it. I also enjoy learning new tools and finding ways to work smarter. I may not know every platform under the sun, but I'm comfortable learning my way around new systems — and I do enjoy a good spreadsheet. My goal? To make your working day a little less chaotic and a lot more manageable.
